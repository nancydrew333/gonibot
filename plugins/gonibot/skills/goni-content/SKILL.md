# GoniBot content duties

Load **goni-core** first. Everything here is written in the **owner's** voice (goni-core
`references/owner-voice.md`), except the short message GoniBot sends with it. Save posts and
newsletters to the notebook's `drafts/` folder, dated. Nothing is posted or sent.

Never treat content as wasted effort. Visibility is assigned on purpose; GoniBot's job is to make
it take less of the owner's time and pair it with a next step for readers.

## Content batch (Waterfall Method)

Phase 4: Waterfall Marketing Method. Phase 6: Email Newsletters.
1. Ask for one 5 to 10 minute voice note (or voice-to-text) about anything: a client win (no
   identifying details), a myth they keep hearing, a question patients ask, something from their
   week. If they don't know what to talk about, offer 3 prompts from the content calendar.
2. From that one note, draft:
   - 3 to 5 posts with captions (mix: education, story or behind the scenes, one with a clear
     offer). Each caption ends with one call to action from the back pocket offers.
   - 2 to 3 story ideas (a poll, a question box, a quick tip).
   - 1 newsletter draft: subject line, warm intro, one key piece of value, one call to action,
     one to two phone scrolls long. Alternate newsletter and promo weeks if they send weekly.
   - The Google Business Profile post (below).
3. Captions: standard grammar, no em dashes, under 2,200 characters, clients by initials only
   with permission.
4. Message: what's ready, and "want me to tweak any before you schedule them?"

## Google Business Profile post

Phase 6: SEO Basics. A short post (100 to 250 words) from the same voice note, with the
practice's location and one search phrase a local patient would use, and a call to action
(book, call, or learn more). Owner posts it.

## Review asks

Phase 4: Google Reviews and Video Testimonials.
1. Ask: "who had a win this week?" First names only.
2. Best timing is 60 to 70% through their plan of care, again at 80 to 90%, then at discharge.
3. Draft a personal ask per person that asks permission first, mentions their specific progress
   in general terms (no clinical details), and includes the review link from the profile.
4. Offer a reminder draft for anyone asked last week who hasn't left one.

## Friday wins

Pull the week's wins from `logs/wins.md`, the lead log, and anything the owner mentioned. Draft a
short Circle post in the owner's voice (initials only for clients). Message: "here's your wins
post if you want to share it. no pressure either way." Celebrate in GoniBot's voice too.

## Content calendar

Phase 5, Lesson 5: Monthly Content and Marketing Planning.
1. Ask for next month's goal and anything they're promoting (an offer, workshop, lead magnet).
2. Draft a 4-week plan using the content matrix categories: authority and education, engagement
   and community, promotional and sales, personal and relatable. 3 posts a week by default
   (12 a month), plus newsletter topics and the promo weeks.
3. Save it to `drafts/content-calendar-<month>.md`.

## Event assistant

Phase 4: How to Host a Sales Event.
Build a checklist across the three phases (promotion, the event, follow-up), a 2-week promo
schedule with post and email drafts, a sign-up form question list (name, email, phone at
minimum), RSVP reminder drafts, and the follow-up sequence (thank-you within 24 hours, personal
follow-ups to each attendee). Save to the notebook.

## Email nurture builder

Phase 6: Email Lead Magnet and Welcome Nurture Sequence.
Draft a welcome sequence for the owner's lead magnet: welcome plus the download link, then
alternating value and "about us" emails, then 3 invitation emails (the last one addressing
hesitation with a softer offer), over 14 to 21 days. 7 to 10 emails. Owner's voice throughout.
Save to the notebook and offer to create Gmail drafts of the first two for review.
