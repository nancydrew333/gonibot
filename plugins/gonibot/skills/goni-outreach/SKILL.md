# GoniBot outreach duties

Load **goni-core** first. Drafts are in the owner's voice. Past clients are first names only and
no clinical details: check in on the person and their goal, never their condition.

Outreach is service, not bothering people. If the owner hesitates, normalize it in GoniBot's
voice: "at the very beginning it was so hard to talk to anybody. that's normal. it gets easier
with reps."

## Outreach list (Wednesday)

Phase 4: Prospecting and Outreach Without Ads.
1. Build a short list (5 to 10 people) from: past clients 30 to 90 days out (from the owner's
   list), referral partners not contacted in 30+ days, warm leads who went quiet, new Instagram
   followers worth a hello, community members.
2. For each, one drafted message with a specific reason to reach out and one ask (a referral, a
   review, a coffee, a consult, or just a check-in). Every connection gets a purpose.
3. If the list is thin, ask the owner for 5 names, any category. Everybody counts.
4. Log sent outreach as connections in `logs/lead-log.md` when the owner confirms.

## 100 Challenge tracker (Foundations)

Phase 4: The 100 Challenge. Phase 6: The Extended 100 Challenge.
Keep a running count in `logs/100-challenge.md` (date started, goal and timeframe: 100 in 10
days, 50 a week, 25 a week, Filthy 50 or Dirty 30). Each Wednesday: count touches so far, draft
the next batch (individual messages, never one mass message), and note which of the five groups
hasn't been reached yet (healthcare professionals, local businesses, online audience, past
clients and leads, community members). Celebrate progress, never the gap.

## Referral partner nurture (mid-month)

Phase 4: Collaborating With Other Businesses. Phase 6: Creating a Referral Program.
Keep `referral-partners.md` in the notebook (name, business, how they're connected, last touch,
what was offered). Each month, draft a check-in per partner that offers value first (a resource
for their clients, a workshop idea, a thank-you for a referral), not "send me clients."

## Past-client reactivation list (monthly)

Phase 3: Developing Your Sales Process (post-discharge check-ins at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months).
1. Ask the owner for past clients they'd like to check on, or read the list they keep (first
   names and discharge month only).
2. Draft a short, personal check-in for anyone at 1, 3, 6 or 12 months out: how they're doing
   with the thing they cared about (running, lifting, chasing their kids), and an open door to
   the maintenance or wellness offer if they have one, or a tune-up visit.
3. Remind the owner this is also the best moment for a review or referral ask.
