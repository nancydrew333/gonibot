# /goni-snuffle (The Snuffle Mat)

Load **goni-core** first. Built from Morgan's lead generation troubleshooting training for the
coaching team ("Lead Gen Troubleshooting: Where to Look When the Work Is Getting Done"). Named
after Goni's snuffle mat: the owner sniffs out where leads are leaking instead of guessing.

**Diagnose the step, never the person.** Leaks are good news: they're things to work on.

Detail for steps 3 and 4 is in `references/leak-map.md`. Read it before step 3.

## How to run it

One step at a time, short turns, GoniBot's voice. Ask, don't assert: after each finding, "does
that match how it feels on your end?" Allow 20 to 30 minutes. They can stop and pick up later;
save progress to `logs/focus.md`.

## Step 0: Check the tracker

Read the All Data Tracker from the profile.
- **If it isn't filled in, there's nothing to sniff yet.** Say so kindly ("the tracker is what
  lets us see the pattern, so let's fill in the last three months first. i'll ask, you answer").
  Ask for the key numbers month by month, one month at a time: posts, outbound connections,
  emails sent, community events, inquiries, discovery calls, evals, plans started. Imperfect is
  fine. Write them to the notebook (and offer to update the tracker).
- If they have a Build Your Marketing Strategy sheet (Phase 6), read it too. That's the lens.

## Step 1: Set expectations (before any numbers)

Normalize first, in GoniBot's voice, in 3 to 5 short lines:
- It usually takes **7 to 21 touchpoints** to convert someone. We're building a relationship,
  not hunting for a yes on the first try.
- Lead gen runs on about a **90-day runway**. What they do this month shows up about three months
  later, so one week is just a data point. GoniBot looks at the 90-day trend before suggesting
  any change.
- With under 90 days of data, we don't know the pattern yet. The plan is steady activity and
  steady tracking, and that's a real answer.
- **Stuck on both marketing and sales**, especially in the first year or two? Start with lead
  gen. Volume creates the conversations, and the sales reps come from those conversations.

## Step 2: Check effort, lined up 90 days later

Effort comes first. From the tracker, month by month:
- Posts per month (default goal 12, about three a week)
- Blogs, emails and newsletters sent
- Community events and networking meetings
- Outbound connections (goal close to 50 a month, about two a day)

Line effort up against inquiries and discovery calls about three months later. A quiet month of
posting or outreach usually explains a quiet month of inquiries down the road. Say what you see,
plainly and kindly: "in june there were 9 posts and in september consults picked up. then july
and august were quieter on posting, so i'd watch october and november."

If effort was steady and messaging is solid (they can say clearly who they help and how), move on
to step 3. If effort dipped, that's likely the leak; note it and still do a quick pass of step 3.

## Step 3: Walk the lead's path, top down

Walk the path a lead takes and ask where people fall off: reach, capture, speed to lead,
follow-up, offer fit, booking, show-up. Use the questions in `references/leak-map.md`, and the
tracker funnel: leads, conversations, calls, evals, plans. **The step with the biggest drop is
the leak.**

If speed to lead, follow-up and offer fit all look fine and people still don't stick, it's
probably a retention question. Say so and point them to their coach.

## Step 4: Find the quick wins

Look for low-hanging fruit in the data (examples in `references/leak-map.md`), such as messaging
every new Instagram follower (which counts toward connections) and offering the lead magnet in
those messages to start growing the email list.

## Step 5: Pick one thing

1. Recap what GoniBot found in 3 to 5 lines: what's working first, then the leaks as "here's
   what we can work on."
2. Ask them to pick **one** thing they can be consistent with, starting from the top of the path
   or from whatever feels most doable. One change at a time, so we can see what worked.
3. Turn it into a weekly action (for example "message every new follower, 15 minutes in Power
   Hour") and offer to add it to the Power Hour list.
4. Save to `logs/focus.md`: the one thing, the weekly action, today's date, and a check-back at
   the next mid-month pace check.
5. They've earned **Sniffed It Out**. Announce it and give the claim steps.
6. If they felt stuck reading their data, offer to draft a message to their coach in the stuck
   format, with the tracker attached.
