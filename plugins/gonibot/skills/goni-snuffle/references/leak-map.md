# The leak map

From Morgan's "Lead Gen Troubleshooting: Where to Look When the Work Is Getting Done" and the
Loom walkthrough for the coaching team.

## Where leads leak

| Where it leaks | What it looks like | What GoniBot asks or checks |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Lots of activity, few new faces | Is the content reaching people who can pay, or mostly peers and existing followers? Which channel actually produced past leads? Are we even talking to the right people? |
| Capture | People engage, then disappear | Are we getting the big four (name, email, phone, Instagram)? A "lead" with only an email, or only an Instagram handle, is hard to keep talking to. Is there a lead magnet or easy next step? |
| Speed to lead | Leads come in, go cold fast | How long until the first reply? Same day, or days later? Goal: within 24 hours when possible. (Morgan's been guilty of five or six days herself, so normalize it.) |
| Follow-up | One or two touches, then nothing | Count touches per lead. Is there a CRM or system, or does follow-up live in their head? Worried about bothering people? Past leads can always be followed up with: after a couple of years most owners have hundreds. |
| Offer fit | Leads say "not right now" | Is the eval the only offer? Are the back pocket offers ready (eval, discovery call, lead magnet, follow me)? Did the conversation ask enough questions for the puzzle pieces to fit, so the offer clearly matches what they told us? "I have an eval for $250" with no fit won't land. |
| Booking friction | Interested, but never books | Recheck offer fit first. Is booking easy? Are there open slots at times patients want? Morgan prefers booking people manually and only sends a link when she's slammed. |
| Show-up | Booked, then no-show or cancel | Are confirmations and reminders going out? Is there a personal touch before the visit? Does the person know what they'll walk away with from the appointment? |

When a lead won't stay, look hardest at speed to lead, follow-up and offer fit first. If all
three look fine, it's probably retention: point to the coach.

## First places to look in the tracker

1. **The 90-day lead trend.** Is the low week a dip or a real slide? Compare to three months back.
2. **Lead sources.** Which channel did leads actually come from, and did that channel's activity
   drop about 90 days ago?
3. **Leads, conversations, calls, evals, plans.** Find the step with the biggest drop. That's the
   leak.
4. **Follow-up count.** How many touches are leads getting before they go quiet?
5. **Offers being made.** Is every lead getting the same offer, or one matched to where they are?

## Effort benchmarks (defaults, not rules)

- Posts: 12 a month (about three a week)
- Outbound connections: close to 50 a month (about two a day). A connection is any outbound
  conversation related to the business.
- Blogs and emails: any steady rhythm. Months of zero here are a common, very fixable leak,
  because blogs help people find the practice on Google and email keeps them close.
- Community events: any. In-person contacts count as connections too.

## Quick wins to look for

- New Instagram followers who never got a hello. Messaging each one counts as a connection, and
  offering the lead magnet in that message starts growing the email list ("want me to send you
  my free guide? just send me your email").
- People met at community events who weren't added to the CRM or messaged afterward.
- Website traffic going up while blogs are at zero: something is already working; a blog or two
  could ride it.
- Past leads and past clients who haven't heard from the owner in 90+ days.
- Discovery calls picking up but conversion dipping: a Sales Gym session (Discovery Call Lab).

## How to talk about it

- **Normalize first.** "a quiet week after a good one is how lead gen works. this is where most of
  us live."
- **Diagnose the step, not the person.** "you're doing the work. let's find where people are
  slipping out."
- **Frame leaks as good news.** "there are 4 or 5 leaks here, and that's actually great news,
  because it means there's stuff we can work on."
- **Pick one leak to fix.** One change at a time, so we can see what worked.
- **Ask, don't assert.** "here's what i'm seeing. does that match how it feels on your end?"
- **Not sure? Bring it to your coach.** Offer to draft the message with the tracker attached.
