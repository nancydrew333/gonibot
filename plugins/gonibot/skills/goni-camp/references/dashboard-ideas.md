# Ideas for growing your home base

Use this after graduation, at the monthly tidy, or any time the owner says "what else can I add",
"ideas for my dashboard", or "level up my HQ". Offer **2 or 3 ideas at a time**, picked to fit
where they are (their phase, what's on their mind, what they use most). Never the whole list.

Each idea has a ready-to-use ask. The owner can say it to GoniBot (for a Claude page) or paste it
into Claude Code (for a dashboard built there). Notion owners can build most of these as Notion
blocks or linked databases.

Same rules for every idea: no patient information (counts and first names of leads only), no
passwords, kind numbers (pace and "to go", never misses), and nothing on the page sends,
posts, books or pays for them.

## Quick wins (5 to 15 minutes)

- **"Today" view.** "Add a section at the top that changes by day: on Power Hour days show my
  follow-ups and outreach links, on clinic days show my booking, intake and exercise program
  links."
- **Pinned favorites.** "Let me star my 5 most-used links so they show up first."
- **Quick copy box.** "Add a box with things I paste a lot: my booking link, review link, lead
  magnet link, and my address for home visits, each with a copy button."
- **Saved replies.** "Add my top 5 saved replies (price question, insurance question, 'what do
  you do?', reschedule, review ask) with copy buttons." Pairs with the FAQ library duty.
- **Brand it.** "Use my brand colors and fonts, and add my logo at the top."
- **Motivation corner.** "Add my 'why' and my 90-day goal in one line at the top."

## Sales and growth (30 to 60 minutes)

- **Follow-up board.** "Add a follow-up board with columns: new lead, talked, eval booked, plan
  started, follow up later. First names only. I'll update it from my lead sheet." Ties to the
  back pocket offers: a no moves them down a rung, not off the board.
- **Touchpoint counter.** "Next to each lead, show how many touchpoints we've had, so I remember
  7 to 21 is normal."
- **Referral partners.** "Add a referral partner list with the last time I checked in and a nudge
  when it's been more than 30 days."
- **Reactivation list.** "Show past clients I haven't checked in with in 90+ days, first names
  only, with a link to my reactivation message template."
- **Review tracker.** "Show my Google reviews count over time and my review ask link."
- **Offer cheat sheet.** "Add a card with my offers, prices and what's included, so I can glance
  at it before a call."
- **Sales Gym corner.** "Add a spot for my call scripts and a link to the Sales Gym, with a
  checkbox for 'practiced this week'."

## Marketing and visibility

- **Content calendar peek.** "Show this week's planned posts from my content calendar."
- **Idea inbox.** "Add a place to jot content ideas that saves on the page."
- **Lead source chart.** "Show where this month's leads came from as a simple bar chart."
- **Lead magnet stats.** "Show lead magnet downloads by month next to new email subscribers."
- **Event planner.** "Add an upcoming workshops and events section with dates, links and a
  checklist for each."

## Numbers and planning

- **The wave.** "Show my last 12 months of revenue, with a 3-month average line, so I can see the
  wave getting smaller." Ties to the 90-day runway: this month's work shows up later.
- **Pace check.** "Show how this month is pacing against my goal, with days left and evals to go."
- **Capacity meter.** "Show visits booked this week against my max, and how many open spots."
- **Quarter view.** "Add my 90-day game plan goals with a simple progress bar for each."
- **Wins wall.** "Pull my wins from GoniBot's logs/wins.md and show the latest 10."
- **Hours back.** "Show how many hours a week GoniBot has taken off my plate, from
  logs/hours.md."

## CEO and team (when they're growing)

- **SOP library.** "Make my SOPs searchable on the page, with a 'last updated' date for each."
- **First hire view.** "Add a section for a future assistant: the links and SOPs they'd need,
  without my numbers." Pairs with the first-hire kit in `/gonibot:goni-train`.
- **Admin and finance checklist.** "Add my monthly admin and finance checklist with checkboxes
  that reset each month."
- **CEO time.** "Show my CEO time blocks this week and what I planned to work on."
- **Shareable view.** "Make a version of this page that hides the numbers so I can share it with
  a VA."

## Make it yours

- **Seasonal touches.** "Change the colors for the holidays" or "add a quote of the week."
- **Personal links.** "Add a small personal section: my gym schedule, my kids' school calendar,
  my book list." It's their HQ for their business and their life.
- **Dark mode switch, bigger text, or a phone layout** they like better.

## How GoniBot offers these

- At graduation (any level): one quick win.
- At the monthly tidy: one idea that fits what came up that month (slow month: reactivation list
  or referral partners; busy month: capacity meter or saved replies; lots of content: lead source
  chart).
- After a level up to the full dashboard: two ideas from Sales and growth or Numbers.
- Ask before building anything, and add one idea at a time. Log each addition in `camp.md`.
