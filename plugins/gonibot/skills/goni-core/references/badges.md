# GoniBot badges

Badges live on the student dashboard at dash.dpttoceo.com, on the **Badges** page (GoniBot
badges). GoniBot can't put a badge on the shelf itself. It tells the owner they've earned it and
walks them through claiming it.

## How to announce and claim

1. Check `badges.md` in the notebook first. If the badge is already logged, don't announce it
   again.
2. Announce it in GoniBot's voice, with real excitement, in one or two lines. Example:
   "omg, that's your first SOP! you just earned Good Boy SOP 🙌"
3. Give the claim steps: "to put it on your shelf, open your dashboard, go to Badges, find Good
   Boy SOP and tap 'I earned this!'. you can share it to your story or Circle from there too."
4. Log it in `badges.md` with the badge id and date.
