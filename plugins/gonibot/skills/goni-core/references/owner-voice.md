# Writing in the owner's voice

Anything that goes to the owner's leads, clients, referral partners or the public is written in
the **owner's** voice, not GoniBot's and not Morgan's. GoniBot is ghostwriting.

## Where the voice comes from

The notebook's `voice-samples.md` holds 3 to 5 samples collected in the interview (voice notes,
voice-to-text, past DMs, captions, emails the owner wrote). Before drafting, re-read them and
note:

- Greeting and sign-off habits ("hey!", "hi friend", first name only, no sign-off)
- Sentence length and punctuation (exclamation points, lowercase, emoji use)
- Words they repeat and words they never use
- How formal they are with leads versus past clients versus providers

If `voice-samples.md` is empty or missing, write warm, plain, professional copy, flag that it's a
generic draft, and ask the owner for one or two samples (a voice note is fine) so the next one
sounds like them.

## Defaults for every client-facing draft

- Standard capitalization and grammar unless the samples clearly do otherwise.
- No em dashes.
- Short. A follow-up DM is two to four sentences. An email fits on one phone screen.
- Always end with one clear, easy next step (one of the back pocket offers), phrased as an
  invitation.
- Use the person's first name. Never include health details for anyone who has booked an
  evaluation, and never mention another client.
- Name the specific thing from the last conversation when there is one ("how did the half
  marathon go?").
- No pressure language, no fake urgency, no "just checking in" with nothing new to say. Each
  touch should give something: a tip, a link, a question about them, or a next step.

## Where drafts go

- Email: create a Gmail draft (never send). Put the recipient, a short subject, and the body.
- DM or text: show the draft in the chat in a quote block with the person's first name above it,
  ready to copy. If GHL is connected, note which conversation it belongs to.
- Social posts, Google Business Profile posts, newsletters: show in the chat, and save to the
  notebook's `drafts/` folder with the date in the file name.

## Learning from edits

When the owner edits a draft before sending, or says "i'd never say that", add a short note to
`voice-samples.md` under "Notes from edits" (for example: "doesn't use 'friend'", "signs texts
with -K"). Keep that section to the most useful 15 notes, merging older ones.
